Pictefor Bridge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 July 2026

Pictefor Bridge is a browser extension that connects the Pictefor desktop application to your browser, so that collecting page screenshots happens in the browser you already use, under your supervision.

What the extension does with data

  • The extension communicates exclusively with the Pictefor application running on your own computer, over a local connection (127.0.0.1). No data is transmitted to Pictefor's servers or to any third party by the extension.
  • When you start a collection in the Pictefor app, the extension captures screenshots and page links from the pages you chose and hands them directly to the app on your computer. Nothing is captured unless you start a run, and you can watch, pause or stop the run at any time.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not collect analytics or telemetry of any kind.
  • It does not track your browsing history.
  • It does not read, store or transmit passwords or other credentials.
  • It does not sell or share any data — it has none to sell.
  • It never acts on its own: every action is a direct response to a command you triggered in the Pictefor desktop app, and any sign-in or human-verification step is always completed by you, never by the extension.

Where captured data lives

Screenshots and extracted links are stored locally by the Pictefor desktop application on your machine and are governed by the Pictefor application's own privacy policy once there. The extension itself stores nothing.
